Create a QR Code
What is a QR code?

A QR code is a two-dimensional barcode that a smartphone camera can scan to quickly open information such as a website, contact, Wi-Fi network or location.

Is it safe to use QR Fusion?

Yes. Core QR generation happens locally in your browser. Optional PIN-code lookup contacts an external postal service, but your QR payload is not uploaded to an application backend.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. QR Fusion creates static QR codes that do not expire. A linked QR continues working as long as its destination remains available.

Why isn't my QR code scanning correctly?

Verify the entered data, increase foreground/background contrast, keep the logo smaller and test the code at its final printed or displayed size.

Which format is best for printing?

SVG is best for unlimited scaling. PDF is convenient for print workflows, while a high-resolution PNG also works for many designs.
