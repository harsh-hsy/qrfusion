Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is a QR code?

A: A QR (Quick Response) code is a barcode that can be scanned by a smartphone camera to quickly access information like a website, contact details, or Wi-Fi credentials.

Q: Is it safe to use QR Fusion?

A: Yes, it's completely safe. Your data is processed entirely within your browser and is never sent to our servers. We do not store any of the information you enter.

Q: Do the QR codes I create expire?

A: No. The QR codes you generate are static and do not expire. They will work forever, as long as the data they point to (like a website link) remains active.

Q: My QR code isn't scanning correctly. What should I do?

A: First, double-check the entered data. If you've heavily customized the design, try increasing the color contrast between the QR code and its background. Ensure your logo doesn't cover too much of the code's pattern.
